# Catalogue and editorial ranking

The committed catalogue is an attributed extraction from the pinned OpenAI math snapshot. Run `python research/build_ranking.py` to regenerate the editorial `ranking.json` from `catalog.json`. The catalogue-extraction program is not part of this public snapshot; the catalogue itself and its counting/provenance metadata are supplied. Scores are editorial heuristics, not proof reliability or predicted views. See `REPORT.md` and `../production_status.json` for scope and current video progress.
